# Pixelmon: Bugs & Ideas

Found a bug in **Pixelmon**, or thought of something cool for the game? This is the place to tell us!

Every report and idea lives here as an [issue](https://github.com/i-am-fyre/Pixelmon-issues/issues). The game's own code is kept somewhere else.

## How to send one

**From the game (easiest):**

1. Open the **Menu** and pick **Bugs & Ideas**.
2. Choose **🐞 Bug** or **💡 Idea**, give it a short title and tell us more.
3. Press **Send on GitHub**. Everything is filled in for you, so just press **Create** on GitHub.

**From this page:** press **New issue** and start the title with `[Bug]` or `[Idea]`, for example `[Bug] I got stuck in a wall in Pallet Grove`.

You need a GitHub account to send one (GitHub accounts are for ages 13 and up).

## Tips for a great bug report

- **What happened**, and what you expected to happen instead.
- **Where you were** and what you were doing just before.
- **Can you make it happen again?** If so, how?
- Keep **"Add game info"** ticked in the game. It adds where you are, your team and your screen size, which helps a lot.
- A **screenshot** helps too. You can paste one straight into the issue.

## Please keep it safe

**Everyone can read what's posted here.** Please don't write your real name, where you live, your school, or anything else personal. The game's "game info" never includes your trainer's name or your creatures' nicknames.

## How reports are sorted

A title starting with `[Bug]` gets the **bug** label, and one starting with `[Idea]` gets the **idea** label. That happens automatically, a few seconds after the report is sent.
